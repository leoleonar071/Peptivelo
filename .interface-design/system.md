# Peptivelo interface identity

Audience: a shopper comparing two serum labels, without chemistry training. Task: recognize two products, see documented differences, then inspect sources. Feel: calm, considered and legible, with uncertainty visible at the point of decision.

Domain: formulation, exact ingredient identity, volume, label disclosures, source trail, observation date. Color world: paper white, petroleum ink, mineral gray, muted verification green and restrained copper. Signature: a paired label notebook where every difference is accompanied by its limit and source, rather than a product score.

Defaults replaced: native select plus a second search field → visual search dialog; submit-and-wait comparison → automatic results after two selections; wide mobile matrix → paired facts within each question. Do not introduce benefits ratings or fabricate missing prices.

Shared component checkpoint: the user-supplied October 2026 manual supersedes the prior DM Sans/Georgia palette. Use src/styles/peptivelo-tokens.css and JSON: ivory/white light theme and petroleum dark theme, semantic pv-* variables plus legacy aliases. Locally hosted Manrope for headings and provisional name, Inter for body/controls/tables, tabular numerals for prices. Controls 12px, cards 18px, large blocks 24px; spacing remains a 4px grid. Original supplied isotype only; no invented reverse/vector. See docs/PEPTIVELO_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md and BRAND_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md. Theme defaults light and persists the user's explicit choice.

Selector intent: recognizable product cards, native modal focus containment, search and normal buttons with keyboard navigation. Thumbnails use the currently published owned editorial product records, with honest alt text and uncropped containment. Do not restore retired manufacturer photographs or generate commercial packaging. On image failure, use a stable label-record fallback. Main trigger has a 44px minimum touch target. Escape/backdrop/close restore focus. Body scrolling is locked only while open.

Comparison intent: two equal product columns with aligned rows for volume, format, price, unit cost, selected names, disclosures, unknowns and sources. Show only differences hides matching recorded facts; missing data, concentration context and source trails remain visible. Detailed explanations use native disclosures below the table. No efficacy summary or winner is invented.

Mobile: full-height search sheet and responsive table rows with a full-width characteristic label followed by two aligned A/B values. Preserve native table/header semantics and avoid horizontal page scrolling at 320px. Root comparator V2: centered picker up to 880px; no sticky product controls. Editorial readers keep their existing matrix presentation. On narrow screens, place each attribute above its aligned A/B values and repeat product identities. Compact the page introduction and action toolbar after both products are selected.

Readability: body 17px, main copy 16–18px, controls 14–16px, supporting text at least 13px. Prices use tabular numerals. Primary controls target 44–48px height. Preserve the visible label in each accessible name.

Home: centered knowledge discovery, with one editorial H1 (maximum 940px), supporting copy (690px) and a universal search (820px, 72px desktop, 64px mobile). Existing Manrope/Inter, ivory surfaces, petroleum action and semantic dark tokens. Suggestions balance four categories with at most two per category; keyboard selection stays in the combobox, with a separate all-results route. Four understated pill links open code scanning/identification, comparison, library and unit-price calculation. Real starter queries and catalog brands link to search. Below: two taller editorial library/guide cards and two compact product/comparison cards, with quiet label-derived previews rather than bottle photography. Cards use 20px radius, 16px gaps and 24–28px padding. Prices retain US context; no unsupported market selector. Keep beta, pending review, source limits and methodology visible. Hero styles remain scoped in src/styles/home-hero.css.

Ingredient learning: every compared name has a visible one-sentence explanation and a native modal with identity, intended use, evidence scope, precautions and an interpretation. Per-section source links are required. Study, supplier, label and safety-review support remain distinct and neutral. Dialogs are fixed within the viewport; mobile sheets occupy the full viewport. Escape/close/backdrop restores trigger focus and body scrolling. Direct guides retain valid comparison product IDs for a return link. Never infer a safety or efficacy ranking from a peptide name or its number.

Premium home refinement: single H1 “Understand peptides. Look beyond the claims.”, 700/500 weight, two desktop lines and maximum 1040px. Eyebrow is “INDEPENDENT PEPTIDE KNOWLEDGE” on a quiet pill. Search radius 24px desktop/20px mobile, one circular magnifier (52px/48px), no left icon, 16px input and short mobile placeholder. Theme-aware hero-halo/search-shadow tokens add subtle depth. Suggestions cap at seven, keep each available category and show at most two per group. Ctrl/Cmd+K only outside editable controls and open modals, at 768px+. Quick actions have compact labels and a two-column layout at tablet/mobile widths. Suggested searches make no popularity claim. Knowledge Preview links to real library context, Copper Tripeptide-1 identity and a documented copper-peptide product; three desktop reading areas become compact mobile rows. Header refinements preserve logo/navigation and use a centered editorial BETA badge and 44px circular theme control.

All editorial comparison readers: three cited buying answers followed by mutually exclusive overview / ingredients and price / evidence tabs. Label-only mode must say that label information is not a clinical review; label paragraphs may cite manufacturers, scientific paragraphs may not. Source types are neutral text, not scores. Technical tables render only with pair-specific records. Keep missing size prices, unverified individual amounts and different study preparations explicit at the affected passage.

## Topic-led information architecture (October 2026)

Audience now includes readers exploring peptide knowledge beyond skincare. The global navigation is Explore topics, Peptides A–Z, Learn and Search. Available topics: Skin & cosmetics, Hair & scalp, Health & research. No empty future categories. Local navigation gives context to direct arrivals; Skincare tools stay inside its section. Existing comparison-reader behavior and brand tokens remain authoritative.
Home order: universal search, topic cards, beginner introduction, four editorial selections with real source-derived review status, methodology and sources. Previous homepage shortcuts, connected preview and full catalog blocks are superseded by this hierarchy. Use quiet paper surfaces, 18px card borders, 24px introductory panels and 4px spacing units. Preserve existing typography and light/dark semantics.
Topic definitions live in src/lib/topic-registry.ts; shared navigation uses only active topics through src/lib/site-navigation.ts. Source-derived content listings live in src/lib/content-navigation.ts. Planned areas have no public href, menu option, search entry or page. Add public topics only when useful content exists. Every content entry needs a stable id, URL, title, description, primary topic and format; additionalTopics can associate the same identity with another area without duplicating its record. Reference editorial status from its source record. Context-specific ingredient profiles stay separate. New index routes are noindex/follow and excluded from the sitemap. Existing URLs and shared tool query parameters stay valid.

## Connected search exploration

The search index preserves document URLs and adds optional entity/context references. Peptide and product suggestions enter a compact exploration in /search/; article suggestions retain their document destinations. Exact names and registered aliases resolve directly; partial terms and questions require explicit choice. Overview / Compare / Evidence show one panel at a time; comparison controls expose Same peptide and Other formulas separately. Exact ingredient IDs determine shared labels; Other formulas requires an existing editorial pair. Texture names do not define compatibility: the current skin catalog is facial serums, kept separate from scalp products.
Keep four alternative cards per page (two columns on desktop, one on mobile), three collapsed evidence entries per page, and six general results per page. A static selection summary holds at most two products and unit prices; never cover mobile content with a fixed action tray. Price sorting uses recorded USD unit costs with missing prices last. Preserve state in URL parameters and native back navigation. No AI-generated explanations or efficacy rankings; source context and pending review remain visible. Shared brand tokens and source-derived language remain authoritative.

## Comparison V2 (root tool)

The root /compare/ tool uses two states: large A/B selection cards before a complete valid pair, then compact identities and an immediate At a glance matrix. One H1 reads “Compare with clarity.”; 56px desktop, 36px mobile, reduced after selection. Use existing Manrope/Inter and semantic petroleum/mineral tokens, a maximum 1200px content width, solid borders and 20–24px card radii. All empty-card space chooses a product. Selection is automatic; controls stay disabled until hydration. Preserve only-B states and native URL history.

Five initially closed groups reveal Product details, Ingredients, Pricing, Brand claims and Evidence. A separate evidence area distinguishes manufacturer labels, ingredient studies, formulation studies and finished-product identity; existing pair-specific passages retain only their associated references. Unknowns, concentration limits, precautions and sources survive differences-only filtering. Manufacturer disclosures use the recorded disclosure text, not editorial interpretations. Dates distinguish price observation from label checks. No efficacy scores or implicit winners.

At 320–600px, attribute labels span each table row above two aligned values. Long detail values stack with explicit A/B labels. No sticky header or floating tray. Exact existing anchors open disclosures before focus, including repeated anchor visits. Legacy editorial readers retain their original tabs and default grid; V2 is an explicit presentation variant, not a replacement for those readers.

## General comparison architecture

Comparison is a global destination: /compare/ and /compare/{slug}/, with Compare in the main menu and Explore footer. Retired skincare comparison routes return 404, without compatibility redirects. Central URL builders live in src/lib/config.ts. The general tool has Home → Compare breadcrumbs, no skincare local navigation, and a compact coverage note: Currently available: facial skincare products. Editorial articles retain their specific titles and a Skin & cosmetics topic link.
Entity kind (product or peptide) is separate from topic. src/lib/comparison-groups.ts registers explicit catalog eligibility and presentation; the only operational group is facial skincare products. Selector, URL validation and connected search share compatibility rules. No empty medical categories, inferred therapeutic equivalence or unstructured peptide comparisons. Future peptide groups need their own reviewed data and presentation adapter.
